Prepared 16 November 2026. These are selected historical cases, not current company diligence. Every record awaits owner identity review. Legal status, current stage and outcomes remain unknown. Source text has been paraphrased; entrant claims are not independently validated.

## IVF support tool

ID: `v_ivf_tool`. Selection: 100k_award.

AI-assisted embryo assessment intended to support IVF treatment decisions. Reported as arising from doctoral research in the Faculty of Medical and Health Sciences.

Award: Velocity $100K Challenge 2025, Place 1.

[Source: Start-ups reach for the stars – literally – in $100k Challenge](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2025/10/17/start-ups-reach-for-the-stars---literally---in--100k-challenge-.html), published 2025-10-17, retrieved 2026-11-16. Locator: First-place IVF team; paragraphs on clinical testing.

**Proposed research angle:** Clinical data access, validation and clinician workflow.

**Example question (not a finding):** What evidence would a clinic need before testing this tool in its workflow?
