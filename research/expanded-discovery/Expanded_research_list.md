## What the wider search changes

- Preserve name history. The proposed Aplenty / Hot Lime Labs link comes from an explicit former-name statement; the existing Zincovery / Zethos link is retained. Neither is an automatic legal-entity merge.
- Keep university research projects and people outside the company table unless a venture identity is established. Licensing is a different relationship from equity investment.
- Read current and exited entries. Record explicit historical outcome reports separately from present operating status.
- Do not attach repeated template status text to every portfolio company, or an earlier parent investment date to a later product spinout.
- Include offshore investors when a dated investment in an eligible NZ venture is supported. Do not import their entire global portfolios as NZ.

In particular, 2000–2010 coverage is still incomplete. No portfolio or the NZ ecosystem has been fully enumerated.

Unknown fields should remain visible rather than being filled from assumption.
